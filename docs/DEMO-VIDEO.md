# Demo video: script and shot list

Rules: under 3 minutes (judges may stop at 3:00), the project running on its intended device,
public on YouTube, and no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music without permission.
Target length: **2:40**. Record at 1080p or higher; a phone on a tripod for the physical shots,
screen capture for the app.

## Recording notes

- Use the AI-generated stand-in face (`docs/ghost/stand-in.jpg`) or Prat's own photo, never anyone else's.
- No background music, or only music we own or that is explicitly licensed for this use.
- Don't show the password, the `.env` file, API keys, or the browser's address bar with credentials.
- Record the voice turns live; if a take fails, re-record rather than editing in audio that didn't happen.
- Keep each spoken line short: the 3-minute cap is strict.

## Reflector buy list (for the physical shots)

The app already renders the right image (pure black stage, mirrored, or four-way for a pyramid);
the reflector just needs to be clear, flat and at 45 degrees.

| Item | What to look for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear acrylic (plexiglass) sheet | 2–3 mm thick, about 30 × 30 cm or larger, protective film on both sides | The single 45° reflector: simplest and brightest setup |
| Or a 4-sided hologram pyramid | Sized for a tablet or laptop screen, clear acrylic | For the four-way pyramid layout |
| Matte black cloth or foam board | Large enough to cover behind and around the reflector | Black behind the glass makes the face float |
| Stand or angle bracket | Holds the sheet at exactly 45° above the screen | Keeps the reflection straight and undistorted |
| Phone tripod | Any | Steady shots of the illusion |

Lay the screen flat facing up, set the sheet at 45° above it, darken the room, turn screen
brightness to maximum, and use Ghost mode with **Mirror reflection** on.
